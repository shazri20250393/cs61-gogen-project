Why we built GoGen

Teenagers share photos, chat with strangers and spend hours on social media, often without knowing what can go wrong.

Advice from parents is not always heard, and many teens find it hard to talk about harassment, scams or how they are feeling. At the same time, unbalanced screen time pushes studies down the list.

GoGen gives them real stories, small rewards and a private way to ask for help.

How it works

Learn

Short articles, videos and real cases explain online risks. Each item shows how long it takes, so a 15-minute read is easy to plan.

Earn

Finishing a safety case, a language lesson or a screen-time goal adds XP and points. Levels and badges keep it fun.

Get rewarded

Parents set the rewards. At 10 points, a teen might choose the family dinner. Parents see progress on their own dashboard.

Learn by travelling, not scrolling

Language lessons and safety articles live on two adventure maps. Finish a stop to unlock the next one and collect XP. Tap the glowing stop to try it.

What’s inside

One platform with two views: one made for teens, one for parents and guardians.

Digital safety content

Articles and real-world cases on privacy, fake accounts, scams and cyberbullying, played as a detective map where each case file unlocks the next.

Language learning map

Short lessons placed along an adventure map. Finish one stop to unlock the next, earning XP and badges on the way.

Goals and reflection

Teens write short-term goals, long-term goals and how they picture life in ten years. Occasional prompts ask what they did today to get closer.

Help when it’s hard to talk

Find official Sri Lankan child-protection and cyber-incident contacts, and reach out in writing instead of on a call.

Progress dashboard

See points earned and the awareness and study activities your child has completed.

Screen-time view

Where the device and its permissions allow it, see daily screen time and the most-used apps.

Screen-time goals

Agree a lower limit for an app, such as two hours of YouTube down to one, and award points when it is met.

Your own rewards

Choose what points are worth: a family dinner, an outing or anything your family agrees on.

Safety and support

GoGen is built so a teenager always has somewhere to turn, and so their information stays theirs.

  • Child protectionNational Child Protection Authority
  • Cyber incidents and online abuseSri Lanka CERT and police cyber-crime reporting channels
  • Emotional distressRecognised national support and counselling services

In-app chat with officials needs each organisation’s agreement. Until then, GoGen shows verified official contact details and reporting channels.

Privacy first

No personal information leaves the system without the user’s consent.

Screen-time and most-used-app data is recorded for the parent’s review, only where the device allows it.

Any parent notification will take the child’s safety and each authority’s procedures into account.

Proposal at a glance

Who it’s for
Teenagers aged 11–16, and their parents or guardians.
The problem
Unsafe device habits and low priority given to studies.
Our solution
A gamified points-and-rewards platform with adventure maps for digital safety and language learning.
Expected impact
Fewer teenagers drawn into cyber-crime or online harm, and more time spent learning.
Purpose
To obtain approval from the relevant authorities.
